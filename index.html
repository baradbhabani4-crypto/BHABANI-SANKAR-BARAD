Project Details

An educational overview of the proposed system.

Project Aim

Study ways to reduce selected pollutants in petrol-vehicle exhaust using suitable treatment stages and monitoring.

Concept Working Flow

Exhaust inlet → proposed treatment chamber(s) → sensor/monitoring unit → outlet. The exact stages and order require engineering design and testing.

Possible Components

Metal housing, internal treatment elements, temperature or gas sensors, electronic controller, wiring and exhaust connections. Final components depend on validated design.

Pollutants to Study

Carbon monoxide (CO), nitrogen oxides (NOx), unburned hydrocarbons (HC), particulate matter (PM) and carbon dioxide (CO₂).

Important: This is a student concept, not a proven pollution-control device. Do not install it on a road vehicle or claim emission reductions until it has been professionally engineered, tested and certified. CO₂ cannot be removed by an ordinary particulate filter.
